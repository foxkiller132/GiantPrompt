⟡ Arcane Automata

Automate magical industry. Outpace the pollution. Endure.

  • Build — open Build, pick a machine, click the grid. Scale up production — every recipe feeds the next.
  • Research is continuous: pick a tech and your factory feeds it over time, so you must build throughput to advance. Watch Items for rates.
  • Machines — click to upgrade (Glyphs), shift-click to overclock, alt-click to slot a Glyph module.
  • Defend — Ward Towers (from the start) and Golemsmith Hubs hold the line. Pollution (Arcane Residue) draws foes; it settles at an equilibrium, so a steady factory holds steady — expanding raises the threat.
  • Purify — toggle the Purifier to spend surplus Glyphs scrubbing residue and earning ascension perks. It competes with research, so choose when.
  • Follow the Goals panel for direction. The run never ends — it deepens.

Space = pause · X = demolish · drag panels to reposition. Progress autosaves; Continue resumes your last run.
