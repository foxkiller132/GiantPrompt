⟡ Arcane Automata

Automate magical industry to purify the zone — but every machine bleeds Arcane Residue, and residue summons ever-deadlier foes.

  • Build — open the Blueprint Library, pick a machine, click the grid.
  • Research — unlock advanced machines at the Research panel.
  • Upgrade — click a placed machine to level it with Glyphs.
  • Defend — Golemsmith Hubs and Ward Towers fend off enemies.
  • Win — channel surplus Glyphs into the Purifier to cleanse the zone.

Space = pause · X = demolish · drag panels to reposition
