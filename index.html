⟡ Arcane Automata

Automate magical industry. Outpace the pollution. Endure.

  • Build — open the Blueprint Library, pick a machine, click the grid.
  • Research — unlock advanced machines at the Research panel.
  • Upgrade — click a placed machine to level it with Glyphs.
  • Defend — Golemsmith Hubs and Ward Towers fend off enemies.
  • Purify — channel surplus Glyphs into the Purifier; each milestone scrubs residue. The run never ends — it only escalates.

Space = pause · X = demolish · drag panels to reposition. Progress autosaves; Continue resumes your last run.
